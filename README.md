# Olist-E-Commerce-Sales-Analysis-using-SQL-Power-BI
End-to-End Data Analysis project using SQL Server and Power BI on the Brazilian Olist E-Commerce dataset. The project covers database design, ETL, data validation, exploratory data analysis (EDA), business insights, and interactive dashboards.



The project includes:
Exploratory Data Analysis
SQL Views
KPI Analysis
Interactive Dashboards



Tools:
SQL Server
Power BI



KPIs:
Total Revenue
Total Orders
Total Customers
Average Order Value
Average Review Score
Customer Segmentation
Seller Performance
Delivery Analysis



SQL Features:
CTE
SUBQUERY
Window Functions
Views
CASE WHEN
Aggregations
Ranking Functions

Business Insights:

1- Customer Retention
Approximately 93.6% of customers made only one purchase, indicating a low customer retention rate and highlighting an opportunity to improve repeat purchases.

2- Seller Revenue Concentration (Pareto Analysis)
Revenue follows the Pareto Principle, where a relatively small group of sellers generates the majority of total revenue.
Recommendation:
Focus on retaining top-performing sellers through incentive programs while helping lower-performing sellers improve their sales to reduce revenue concentration risk.

3- Product Category ABC Analysis
Product categories were classified using the ABC Analysis technique.
Category A contributes to approximately 80% of total revenue.
Category B contributes to the next 15%.
Category C contributes to the remaining 5%.
This helps the business prioritize inventory, marketing, and supplier management.

4- Revenue by State
(SP) is the largest revenue-generating state, contributing approximately 38.33% of the total revenue.
The top 2 states (SP and RJ) account for over 51.64% of total revenue.
The top 7 states generate approximately 81.50% of the total revenue, indicating that revenue is highly concentrated in a small number of states.
This concentration suggests that marketing campaigns, logistics improvements, and inventory planning should prioritize these high-performing states.

5- Delivery Performance
Approximately 92% of delivered orders arrived earlier than the estimated delivery date, demonstrating strong delivery performance and efficient logistics.

6- Customer Segmentation
Most customers belong to the Medium and Low Value segments, while High Value and VIP customers represent a small portion of the customer base but generate significantly higher average spending.

7- Revenue Trend
Revenue experienced strong growth during late 2017, followed by a noticeable decline in December, suggesting seasonal purchasing behavior after peak sales periods.
